Meet Your Teacher

Hi, I'm Hailey!

I believe learning the piano should be an inspiring, positive experience—never an intimidating one. My studio is designed to be a comfortable space where students feel encouraged to explore, make mistakes, and discover the music that excites them.

Whether you're looking for lessons for your child or wanting to begin or pick up the piano as an adult, we'll design each lesson around what keeps you motivated, confident, and having fun at the keys.

My Approach

What you can expect from me.

Every student learns differently. My job is to meet students where they are while helping them build the skills and confidence to keep progressing.

♫

Learn at Your Pace

Students shouldn't feel like they're racing through a curriculum. We'll slow down when something needs work and move forward when they're ready.

★

Confidence First

Mistakes are part of becoming a musician. Lessons should be a comfortable place to try, struggle, improve, and celebrate progress.

♪

Music Should Be Fun

Technique and theory matter, but so does enjoying what you play. We'll work toward strong fundamentals without losing the joy that makes music worth learning.
